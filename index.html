FAQ

Common questions

I have never studied a foreign language. Can I start?

Yes. Every language has a complete beginner path. A short level check in your demo class places you correctly.

Which exams do you prepare for?

JLPT for Japanese, Goethe-Zertifikat for German and DELF and DALF for French, with mock tests and exam strategy.

Are the mentors native-level?

Our classes are led by native-level mentors who teach pronunciation, culture and real usage, not just textbook grammar.

Do you help with studying or working abroad?

Yes. Beyond the language, we guide you on certification planning, documents and interview preparation for your destination.

How much do courses cost?

Fees depend on the language, level and batch. Book a free demo class and we will share the details with you.
